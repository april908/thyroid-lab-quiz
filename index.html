GI Map

You're doing everything right.
The problem is in your gut.

The root cause you haven't tested yet.

When you're eating clean, managing stress, sleeping well, and still feel terrible — that's not a willpower problem. That's a root cause problem. And for women with Hashimoto's who are "doing everything right," the most common missing piece is gut health.

About 70–80% of your immune system lives in your gut. Hashimoto's is an autoimmune condition, which means if your gut lining is compromised, pathogens are present, or your microbiome is dysregulated — your immune system stays in overdrive no matter how clean your diet is. A GI Map is a comprehensive stool test that gives us a clear DNA-based picture of your gut bacteria, yeast, parasites, inflammatory markers, and intestinal permeability.

This test doesn't just tell you what's wrong — it tells us exactly where to start healing. And for women who've hit a plateau, it's often the test that changes everything.

Your hormones aren't just "off."
They're undermining everything.

Your thyroid and hormones are in a tug-of-war — and you're losing.

The connection between thyroid function and sex hormones is real and deeply underappreciated. High estrogen dominance can actually bind thyroid hormone in your bloodstream, making it unavailable for your cells — even when your labs look "normal." Progesterone deficiency makes this worse. And cortisol dysregulation throws the entire endocrine system into chaos.

The DUTCH Complete test is the gold standard for assessing your full hormonal picture — estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, cortisol rhythms, and their metabolites. It goes far beyond a simple blood hormone panel to show not just your hormone levels, but how your body is processing and clearing them, which is where so many women get stuck.

If your symptoms are worse around your period, you're exhausted no matter how much you sleep, or your mood is swinging in ways that feel out of your control — this test is built for you. It's often the piece that finally makes the whole puzzle make sense.
